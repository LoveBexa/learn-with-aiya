Why a classroom?

Screens
don’t raise

R/01

A teacher in the room

Stuck is temporary when someone can walk over, look at your screen, and un-stick you in thirty seconds. No forums. No “did you try turning it off?”

R/02

Small cohorts, real pace

Twelve seats per workshop. We move at the speed of the room, not the speed of a pre-recorded video you’ll pause forty times.

R/03

You leave with a thing

Every workshop ends with something built by you, working, live. Not a certificate of attendance — an actual artifact you made.

R/04

People like you

Marketers, teachers, founders, retirees, the AI-curious. Nobody will ask if you’ve “tried the terminal.” Everyone starts where you are.
