Beyond the Prompt

Go beyond prompting. Learn how to build useful tools with AI.

  • 01

    Build something real

    Build a working meal planning tool alongside the instructor using AI.

  • 02

    Learn the method

    Learn a simple re-usable approach after building this to turn future ideas and problems into useful AI tools.

  • 03

    Take it further

    Leave with a usable first version of your tool and the confidence to keep experimenting and build your next one.

If you've only used ChatGPT or Claude to write emails, generate ideas, create images or answer questions, this is your chance to see what happens when you start building with AI instead.

This is a taster introductory workshop for beginners who have dabbled with AI already but want to take it further. We'll guide you through the process of turning an idea into something usable — using the meal-planning tool as the example. You'll learn how to:

  • Break a problem down into something AI can help build
  • Give AI the right instructions to create something useful
  • Work with AI to build and change your tool
  • Test, troubleshoot and improve what you've made
  • Take the same approach and apply it to other ideas

The goal isn't to become a developer. It's to realise you can build useful tools with AI to make your life and work easier.

What you'll actually make

A simple AI meal-planning tool that can:

  1. Build your recipe catalogue

    Find recipes you like online and build your own personal catalogue of meals.

  2. Build your weekly meal plan

    Use your recipe catalogue to create a meal plan for the week based on what you want to eat.

  3. Generate your shopping list

    Turn your meal plan into a complete shopping list with the ingredients you need for the week.

Who's it for?

  • You've only used AI for promptingYou use ChatGPT or Claude for emails, copy, research and images, but haven't explored what else you can create.
  • You have a problem you'd love to make easierMaybe it's something repetitive at work, in your business or everyday life.
  • You're curious about building with AIYou don't want to become a Pdeveloper. You just want to know what's possible — and have a go yourself.

What you need

  • 💻

    A laptop

    You'll be building during the workshop, so bring your laptop.

  • 🤖

    ChatGPT Pro / Claude Pro

    You'll need an active ChatGPT Pro or Claude Pro subscription, set up on your laptop, before the workshop.

No coding experience required.
