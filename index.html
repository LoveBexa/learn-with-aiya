R/01

Real people. Real help.

AIYA is built for busy people. When you're stuck, someone walks over, answers your question and gets you moving again... no endless tutorials or Youtube rabbit holes.

Twelve seats per workshop. Build alongside other beginners, share ideas, ask questions and learn together in a collaborative environment.

Every workshop ends with something you've actually made: a website, AI assistant or workflow you can keep using. No theory without practice.

Everyone starts somewhere.

Whether you're a founder, consultant, teacher or simply AI curious, you belong here. AIYA exists to help people build confidence with AI and not feel left behind.
